Four functions sit inside Tech:

:::defs
items:
  - term: Autonomy
    text: The software that lets a robot understand its surroundings and drive itself — navigation, perception, localization and safety. See [[robot]].
  - term: Electronics
    text: The physical circuit boards, sensors and wiring on and around the robot.
  - term: Validation
    text: Testing robots and software against real and simulated conditions before they are trusted in production. See [[v-validation|Validation]].
  - term: Cloud
    text: The backend services, infrastructure and data pipelines a fleet of robots and Ati’s software depend on.
:::
