id: v-point-station
term: Point station
kind: term
simple: A station that represents one pickup or drop-off point, rather than a multi-cell staging area.
technical: In the v3 PRD, handling granularity does not apply to a Point Station because it has no cells to enumerate. In v2, Point Station is one of the locations that can use auto or manual pickup confirmation.
