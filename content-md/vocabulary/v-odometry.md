term: Odometry
kind: jargon
simple: Estimating how far and in which direction the robot has moved based on its own movement.
technical: An estimate of robot motion derived from onboard sensors such as wheel encoders, inertial sensors, or other motion measurements. Odometry accumulates error over time and is therefore commonly combined with other localization sources.
