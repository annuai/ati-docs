ATI Flow glossary

The vocabulary a new team member needs before reading product specs, designs or deployment conversations.

AMR

Autonomous Mobile Robot. A mobile robot capable of navigating and performing transport work without continuous manual driving.

Robot

The preferred product-facing term for an individual AMR in the current IA.

Fleet

A coordinated group of robots operating within a deployment.

Zone

A geographical operating area used to organize access and operational responsibility.

Processing zone

A configured area associated with a material-processing or operational context. The exact ATI data model should be confirmed before treating it as identical to a generic geographical zone.

Map

The spatial substrate used for localization and operational behaviour.

Map annotation

Operational information layered onto the raw map: stations, behavioural zones, traffic controls, forbidden areas and preferred areas.

Workflow

The configured logic for transport behaviour.

Mission

An executable transport behaviour composed from actions.

Taxi

In the current deployment document, an on-demand, point-to-point transport pattern.

Milk run

A fixed-loop transport pattern with multiple stops.

Bus

A scheduled, repeating transport route.

Fleet Monitor

The live operational view of robots, tasks and traffic, organized around zones.

Maintenance

A robot state/operational condition used to remove a robot from normal dispatch while it is being serviced. The exact ATI implementation and permissions should be confirmed.

Taxi mode

Not established as a universal AMR industry term in the provided documentation. Do not assume it means maintenance, teleoperation or manual driving without confirming ATI’s implementation.

Debug

A low-level configuration and diagnostics area assigned to the Configurator role in the current IA.
