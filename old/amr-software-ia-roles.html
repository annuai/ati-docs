AMR platform — information architecture & roles

Updated page names, a new Robots page, and role-based visibility across four roles: Operator, Fleet Supervisor, Supervisor (Head of Operations), and Solutions Architect (Configurator).

Pages

Current naming, reflecting product terminology in use today.

PageCovers
MapsSLAM mapping, positions, zones, traffic/gate rules
WorkflowsMission and action design, mission patterns, priority rules
Fleet MonitorLive, zone-based view of robots, tasks, and traffic (zone = a geographical area)
RobotsRobot setup — add, edit, delete, name, assign to zone
IntegrationsERP connections, master data mapping, sync logs
Setup & ConfigSite, network, docks, user & role management
DebugLow-level configuration and diagnostics — Configurator only

Roles

Operator

Floor-level, single-zone view

The person physically on the floor, day to day.

  • Fleet Monitor for their own zone — robot status, task queue
  • Can raise a manual priority request, with the trade-off shown before confirming
  • View-only on Robots in their zone — status and location, nothing to edit

Not bothered with:

  • Maps, Workflows, Integrations, Setup, Debug — entirely hidden
  • Other zones' traffic or task queues
  • Any configuration language — zones, missions, master data are invisible here
Fleet Supervisor

Zone-level oversight

Owns one or more zones — the operational layer above a single operator.

  • Fleet Monitor across their assigned zone(s), with reassignment control
  • Manage Robots within their zone — mark for maintenance, reassign
  • View-only on Maps and Workflows, to understand what's configured without editing it

Not bothered with:

  • Integrations, Setup & Config, Debug — entirely hidden
  • Editing missions or map zones — that's a Configurator task
  • Zones outside their own assignment
Supervisor (Head of Operations)

Site-wide operational authority

Sees across every zone — the escalation point above Fleet Supervisors.

  • Fleet Monitor across all zones
  • Manage Robots site-wide — add, rename, reassign zone
  • View and approve Workflows and Maps, without doing the detailed editing
  • View Integrations status — whether syncs are healthy, without configuring them
  • Manage users and roles in Setup & Config

Not bothered with:

  • Debug — entirely hidden
  • Low-level robot parameters (drive tuning, safety calibration) — that's Configurator territory
  • Writing mission logic or drawing map zones from scratch
Solutions Architect (Configurator)

Full configuration authority

Designs the solution — routes, zones, missions, integrations — and is the only role with Debug access.

  • Full edit on Maps and Workflows
  • Full setup on Robots, including low-level parameters
  • Full configuration on Integrations and Setup & Config
  • Debug — raw robot state, calibration, overrides, diagnostic logs
  • View-only on Fleet Monitor — for verifying a configuration behaves correctly, not for daily operations

Not bothered with:

  • Day-to-day task queues or manual dispatch — that's operational, not configuration
  • Zone-specific operational escalations
